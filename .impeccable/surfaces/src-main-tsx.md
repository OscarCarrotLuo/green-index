# Atlas and city exploration

Mode: Experience on the atlas; Operate inside a city. Desktop is the shipped device class; test 1440px and the actual browser width. English content, all 20 MSA deep links and honest pending data are preserved.

## Direction contract
THESIS: A spatial atlas of living cities. Geographic dots locate each city; translucent archival plates let the visitor handle it. Replace the former oversized marketing hero and heavy rounded dashboard blocks with a deliberate exhibition surface.
OWN-WORLD: ClauseOS is the user's pinned world. Ink-black blue-green, silver transparent glass, Harmony Green active material, Urbanist geometric lettering, fine lit edges, recessed pills, and uninterrupted satellite imagery.
STORY: Locate a city nationally, turn its physical archive toward the viewer, then enter its satellite landscape and reveal solar, building or vegetation layers. Keep research status readable without invented values.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Floating brand and inset navigation above a wide dotted national map. The title sits at the left, the map occupies two-thirds. A selected-city locator sits below. The next section is a full-width spatial rack; selected plate faces forward, a slim green dossier joins map and stack. The index becomes a legible lower reading surface.
FORM: User-pinned ClauseOS supersedes assigned candidate 4 from seed aaea5eda. Code-first confirmed. The signature interaction is a damped continuous cyclic rack: drag to scrub, release to settle, center file turns out, click enters the map. Direction constraints were confirmed by the user; no alternative world is substituted.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

Cross-surface: city tools become slender optical-glass controls, lenses become a horizontal floating rail, and one bottom-aligned insight surface supports the map. Data/methodology use the same typography and material.
